Privacy policy

This website is operated for MKJ Children Upper Primary School, Jodhpur. We keep the public website intentionally simple and avoid collecting personal information unless a family chooses to contact the school directly.

Information this website collects

The current website does not use an account system, online admissions database, advertising tracker, or analytics service. The quick admissions enquiry form does not send information to a website server. It prepares an email on your own device so that you can choose whether to send it to the school.

When you contact the school

If you call, email, or otherwise contact MKJ Children Upper Primary School, the school may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, child's class or level of interest, and your enquiry. That information is used to respond to your request and support the admissions or school-enquiry process.

Hosting and third-party resources

The website is hosted through Hostinger, which may process ordinary server and security logs needed to deliver and protect the site. During the preview stage, the website also loads typography from Google Fonts and temporary preview photography from Unsplash. Those providers may receive standard technical connection information such as an IP address, browser details, or request metadata under their own privacy practices. Final MKJ photography is intended to replace the temporary preview imagery.

Cookies and tracking

The current MKJ website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics, embedded maps, videos, or other third-party services are added later, this policy should be reviewed and updated before those services are enabled publicly.

Children's information

Parents and guardians should avoid sending sensitive information about a child through an ordinary website enquiry. For documents, health information, identification records, or other sensitive admissions material, contact the school directly and use the process provided by school staff.

Links to other services

The website may link to phone, email, Google Maps, or other external services. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices.
